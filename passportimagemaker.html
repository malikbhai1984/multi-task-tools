💡 Professional Tips for Perfect Passport Photos

  • Use Good Lighting: Take your photo in well-lit conditions with natural light from the front. Avoid harsh shadows on your face or background.
  • Neutral Expression: Keep a neutral facial expression with both eyes open and mouth closed. No smiling, frowning, or raised eyebrows.
  • Face the Camera Directly: Look straight at the camera with your full face visible. Keep your head straight, not tilted.
  • Plain Background Helps: While our AI removes any background, starting with a plain wall (any color) ensures the best results.
  • Remove Accessories: Take off glasses (unless medically required for daily wear), hats, headbands, and large jewelry that might cover your face.
  • Wear Appropriate Clothing: Wear normal street attire. Avoid uniforms, white clothing (blends with background), or low-cut tops.
  • Correct Head Position: Ensure your head takes up 70-80% of the photo height. Your eyes should be positioned about 2/3 from the bottom.
  • Recent Photo Required: Use a photo taken within the last 6 months that accurately represents your current appearance.
  • No Filters or Editing: Upload original, unedited photos. Don't use beauty filters, photo editing, or retouching before upload.
  • High Resolution: Use a photo with at least 600x600 pixels for best quality. Modern smartphone cameras work perfectly.
